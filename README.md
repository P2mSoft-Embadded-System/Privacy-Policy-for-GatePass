# Privacy Policy for GatePass

**Effective Date:** May 05, 2026

**Project 2morrow Software Limited** ("we", "us", or "our") operates the **GatePass** mobile application (the "Application"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Application and the choices you have associated with that data.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
To provide a seamless attendance and gate pass management experience, we collect several types of information for various purposes.

#### Types of Data Collected:
*   **Personal Identification Information:** While using our Application, we may collect personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, such as:
    *   Full Name
    *   Official Email Address
    *   Employee ID / Code
    *   Profile Picture (Face Data)
    *   Department and Designation
*   **Usage Data:** We may collect information on how the Application is accessed and used.

---

### 2. Device Permissions and Their Purpose
The Application requires specific permissions to function correctly as an enterprise attendance system. We use these permissions only as described below:

*   **Camera (`CAMERA`):** Used exclusively for AI-based face recognition to mark attendance and verify user identity. We do not use the camera for any background activity.
*   **Location (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`):** We collect your location data to verify that attendance or visit tasks are being performed within authorized geographical boundaries. This data is only collected while the Application is in use (foreground).
*   **Network & Internet (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`):** Used to synchronize attendance records with our secure cloud servers and to ensure a stable connection during data transmission.

---

### 3. Data Processing and Security
*   **Face Data:** Your face data is captured solely for identity verification. It is processed securely and is never shared with third-party advertisers or unauthorized entities.
*   **Data Security:** We use industry-standard security measures, including hardware-backed encryption (**EncryptedSharedPreferences**) to store sensitive authentication tokens on your device. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
We may use third-party services (such as Google Play Services and Google Maps API) to facilitate our Application's features. These third parties have access to your data only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

---

### 5. Data Retention
Project 2morrow Software Limited will retain your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy or as required by your employer's internal policies.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
Our Application is an enterprise tool and does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
